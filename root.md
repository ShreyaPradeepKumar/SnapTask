## Rule #1: Simple English Explanations

Every time I ask you to do something — whether we are in **plan mode** or **build mode** — you must:

1. **First, explain what you are going to do in simple English**, as if I am a beginner to software development.
2. **Wait for my approval.**
3. **Do the task only after I approve.**
4. **Give a summary of what you did — again in simple English.**

The idea is: I always need a simple-English explanation for whatever you do. This rule always applies and must never be skipped.
